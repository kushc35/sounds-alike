Do these songs sound alike?

We're testing music recommenders for a statistics course project (STAT 453). You'll see a few songs someone likes, then six suggested songs. Listen and rate how similar each suggestion sounds to the songs at the top.

  1. First, pick 3 or more genres you like. The songs you rate come from those genres.
  2. 12 short rounds, 6 songs each. About 25–30 minutes.
  3. Play a few seconds of each song. You don't need to hear it all.
  4. Judge the sound and feel (instruments, energy, mood, style), not whether you like it.
  5. You can stop and come back later in the same browser. Your progress is kept.
Rating scale
1Not similar
2A little
3Somewhat
4Quite
5Very similar

Ratings are anonymous: we store a random ID, your ratings and how long each round took. No name, email or account. Results are used only for the course report.
